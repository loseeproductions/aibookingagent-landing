Connect Calendly so your agent can book

Booking appointments is your agent's whole job — and for that it needs your calendar. This quick guide gets Calendly set up so every qualified lead lands as a real appointment on your schedule.

📅 Calendly · Scheduling

⏱️ About 10 minutes📄 Have ready: the calendar you already use

Why we need this

Your agent turns conversations into booked appointments. When a lead is qualified, the agent offers your real open times and drops the confirmed appointment onto your calendar — no back-and-forth, no double-bookings.

Calendly connects to your calendar, so you'll create it, then simply send us your booking link and we wire it into the agent. You never share your password.

Step-by-step

1

Create your account

Go to calendly.com and click Sign up. If you use Gmail, choose Sign up with Google — it's fastest and connects your calendar in one step.

💡The free plan is plenty to start.
2

Connect your calendar

Choose the calendar you already use — Google, Outlook/Microsoft, or iCloud — and sign in to allow access. This lets the agent see your real availability and add new bookings automatically.

✅Calendly only reads when you're busy vs. free — it never shares your event details.
3

Set your availability

Tell Calendly the days and hours you accept appointments. The agent will only ever offer times inside those windows.

4

Create your appointment type

Click Create → Event Type. Name it clearly (e.g. "Consultation — 30 min"), set the length, and choose the location (phone, in-person, or video).

5

Send us your booking link

This is all the agent needs. On your event, click Copy link — it looks like calendly.com/your-name/consultation.

🔗Send it to tim@aibookingagent.co and we'll connect it to your agent.
🚫No password needed. On a paid plan and want us to fine-tune it? You can instead add tim@aibookingagent.co as a team member.

✔ What to send us when you're done

  • Your Calendly booking link (e.g. calendly.com/your-name/…).
  • Which calendar you connected.
  • The appointment type(s) you want the agent to book.
