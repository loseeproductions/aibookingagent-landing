Add the chat widget to your website

This is your agent living on your site — greeting visitors, answering questions, and booking appointments 24/7. There are two easy ways to add it. Pick whichever fits you.

💬 Website · Chat Widget
Tip: use Print → Save as PDF to keep or share this guide.
⏱️ About 10 minutes📄 Have ready: access to your website (or your web person)

Why we need this

The widget is where most leads meet your agent. A small chat bubble appears in the corner of your site; when a visitor clicks it, your agent greets them, answers questions, and books them — even at 2 a.m.

It's a tiny, fast snippet that won't slow your site down. You can paste it yourself or add us to your site and we'll install it. You never share your password.

Step 1 · Pick how you want to add it

★ Fastest if you can edit your site
Option A — Paste it yourself
We send you one line of code; you drop it into your site. Works with Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, Shopify, GoDaddy, and most builders.
Hands-off
Option B — We install it
Add us to your website builder as an editor and we'll place the widget for you. Nothing for you to touch.
Option A

Paste the snippet yourself

1

Get your snippet from us

We'll email you a single line of code that looks like this:

<script src="https://widget.aibookingagent.co/agent.js" data-id="YOUR-ID" async></script>
2

Paste it into your website

Where you paste it depends on your builder — in every case, it goes in the site's header or a custom-code / embed block:

• Wix: Settings → Custom Code → Add → paste into <Head>
• Squarespace: Settings → Advanced → Code Injection → Header
• WordPress: a "Header & Footer" plugin, or Appearance → Theme File Editor → header
• Shopify: Online Store → Themes → Edit code → theme.liquid <head>
• GoDaddy / other builders: look for an "Embed" or "Custom HTML" section

💡Not sure where it goes on your builder? Send us your site link and we'll point to the exact spot — or just use Option B.
3

Save & publish

Save your changes and publish the site. Refresh your homepage — the chat bubble should appear in the bottom corner.

Option B

Add us and we'll install it

1

Invite us to your website builder

In your builder's settings, add tim@aibookingagent.co as an editor / collaborator / admin:

• Wix: Site → Roles & Permissions → Invite
• Squarespace: Settings → Permissions → Invite Contributor (Admin)
• WordPress: Users → Add New → role "Administrator"
• Shopify: Settings → Users → Add staff

2

Tell us it's ready

Email us your website address and confirm you invited us. We'll place the widget, test it, and let you know when it's live.

✅You stay the owner. We're just added as an editor to place the widget — you can remove us anytime.
🚫Either way: never send us your website password. A collaborator/editor invite (Option B) or pasting the snippet (Option A) is all we need.

✔ What to send us when you're done

  • Which option you chose (A or B).
  • Your website address.
  • If Option A: confirm you pasted & published. If Option B: confirm you invited tim@aibookingagent.co.
Questions? Email tim@aibookingagent.co — or ask us to hop on a quick call and we'll add it together.
